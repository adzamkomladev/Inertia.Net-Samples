# CarParker critique, round 2 (source review, critic A)

> DEGRADED: single-context source review by critic A. No browser, the app was not run, the detector was not run by me. Every finding comes from reading source in `samples/CarParker/src/CarParker/ClientApp`, plus `Emails.cs` and `ReceiptDocument.cs`. Contrast ratios are computed from the oklch tokens in `app.css`, with alpha tints composited in oklab (Tailwind `color-mix`). Phone widths are worked out from the class arithmetic, not measured.

Target: the whole app, phone first (360 to 393 px). Modes: Operate (driver flows, guard, admin), Read (Policy/About), Persuade (Home).

## Design specificity verdict

The driver surfaces (lot list, lot detail, BookingFlow, account) clearly belong to this product. They use the Spot Code bay map, plate faces, ochre that only appears where it means something, and lot-local time. The guard console is the next most distinctive surface. Admin falls back to generic shadcn styling, with bordered cards, raw strings and UTC timestamps, which reads like a template. The phone check is the one that hurts: three P1s are phone-only problems (Policy gutter, bay inspector placement, guard incident description).

## Findings

### P0
None this round. The two closest candidates are P1-1 and P1-6. Neither corrupts data or blocks a primary task outright, but P1-6 can attach a note to the wrong claim and should be fixed first among the admin items.

### P1

**P1-1. Policy/About page has no gutter or top padding on phones.**
- `pages/about/Policy.svelte:25`: `<article class="prose-parking mx-auto max-w-3xl">` has no `px-*` or `pt-*`.
- StoreLayout `<main>` adds no padding, so at 360 px the body copy and headings touch the screen edges.
- Fix: `class="prose-parking mx-auto max-w-3xl px-4 pt-6 pb-16 sm:px-6"`, matching the other store pages' container.

**P1-2. Plain `<a>` internal links do full page reloads.**
- Sites:
  - `lots/Show.svelte:42`
  - `lots/Book.svelte:31`
  - `components/BookingFlow.svelte:459`
  - `admin/Users.svelte:62`
  - `admin/User.svelte:16`
  - `admin/Team.svelte:71`
  - `admin/Claim.svelte:20,59`
  - `admin/Booking.svelte:35,51`
  - `about/Policy.svelte:119`
  - `layouts/GuardLayout.svelte:80`
- Evidence: each one is an `<a href="/...">` to an Inertia route. A click reloads the whole document. The boot shell flashes, the SignalR connection in `lib/realtime.svelte.ts` is torn down and rebuilt (with a 500 ms start delay), the nav skeleton never shows, and client-rendered pages pay full LCP again (about 1.4 to 1.8 s).
- Fix: use `Link` from `@inertiajs/svelte` with `prefetch="hover"` where DataTable already does. Add a lint check, for example a grep in the verify skill for `<a href="/` in `ClientApp`.

**P1-3. The guard console shows every time in UTC.**
- Evidence:
  - `pages/guard/Console.svelte:73,143,159,207,213,275,298` call `fmtTime(...)` with no `tz`. `lib/time.ts:46` defaults `tz = 'UTC'`.
  - Lines 207 and 213 append a literal "UTC".
  - `LotOption` (`types/index.d.ts:81`) has no `timeZoneId`.
- Impact: a guard at a New York lot reads entry and expiry times 5 hours off. Guards do not reason in UTC.
- Fix:
  - Add `timeZoneId` to `LotOption` and the server projection.
  - Derive `tz` from the selected lot.
  - Pass it to every `fmtTime` or `fmtDayTime` call.
  - Replace the "UTC" suffix with `zoneLabel(tz)` once in the header, not per row.

**P1-4. Guard overstay logic is frozen and measured from the wrong point.**
- Evidence:
  - `Console.svelte:54`: `const now = $derived(Date.now())` is evaluated once and never updates, so `ready()` and `overdue()` stay stale until a live-prop refresh happens to re-render the page.
  - `Console.svelte:78` computes overdue as `entered + grace.overstay minutes`. That is not booking window end plus grace. `SessionRow` carries no window end.
- Impact: any car parked longer than about 20 minutes shows a destructive "Resolve overstay", including a driver who is legitimately inside a 4-hour booking.
- Fix:
  - Add `windowEnd` (nullable for walk-ins) to `SessionRow`.
  - Compute overdue as `now > windowEnd + grace`.
  - Drive `now` from a shared ticking store, for example `setInterval(() => now = Date.now(), 30_000)` in `onMount`.
  - Reuse that store in `RelativeTime`, which also never ticks.

**P1-5. Guard incidents on a phone hide what happened.**
- Evidence:
  - `pages/guard/Incidents.svelte:125-126` mark the description and plate columns `desktopOnly`. On a phone the guard sees a type and a time, then taps "Resolve".
  - Lines 97-101: Resolve has no confirmation, no processing or disabled state (so a double tap posts twice), and uses the destructive (red) variant for a positive action.
- Fix:
  - Make the description the phone `primary` column and show the plate with `PlatePill`.
  - Make Resolve an outline or primary button with `disabled={processing}` and a spinner, plus a short confirm or an undo toast.

**P1-6. Admin Insurance shares one note across every claim row.**
- Evidence:
  - `pages/admin/Insurance.svelte:35,84` bind the same `decideForm.note` in every row. A note typed for claim A shows under claims B and C and is sent with whichever one is approved next.
  - Reject (line 89) fires immediately with no confirmation.
  - The status key `'under_review'` (lines 55,76) does not match `underreview` in `lib/status.ts:42`, so the badge falls back to the default tone.
- Fix:
  - Use per-row state (`notes: Record<id, string>`), or move decide into the claim detail page.
  - Confirm Reject with ConfirmDialog, stating the claim and amount.
  - Normalise the status key in one place.

**P1-7. Admin "Cancel booking" has no confirmation.**
- Evidence: `pages/admin/Booking.svelte:103` posts at once. The action is irreversible: it frees the bay, notifies the driver and files a refund. The customer cancel on `account/Booking.svelte` uses ConfirmDialog with the amount.
- Fix: reuse ConfirmDialog with "Cancel B-XXXX for {driver}? A refund of {amount} will be filed." and a processing state.

**P1-8. Admin bay inspector opens off-screen on phones.**
- Evidence: in `pages/admin/Lot.svelte:99-115`, below `xl` the inspector panel renders after the whole spot board. Tapping a bay changes nothing visible near the bay, and the result is several screens below.
- Fix: below `xl`, render the inspector in a bottom Sheet (`side="bottom"`), or `scrollIntoView({ block: 'nearest' })` and move focus to its heading. Also make the close button 44 px (line 121, currently `size-9`).

**P1-9. Dark-mode contrast fails on tinted callouts.**
- Root cause: the dark `--spot-free-foreground` (L 0.20) and `--cta-foreground` (L 0.25) are inks meant for solid fills, but they are used as text and icons on 15 to 25% tints over dark surfaces.
- Sites (see the contrast table below for the measured ratios):
  - Overstay card: `account/Index.svelte:57,64`.
  - "Daily max applied" note: `BookingSummary.svelte:110`.
  - Pills: `admin/Refunds.svelte:77`, `admin/Insurance.svelte:54`.
  - Queue icon: `admin/Index.svelte:63`.
  - Alert tint icons: `AlertList.svelte:39-40`.
  - Positive trend: `StatCard.svelte:39`.
  - Other sites: `auth/VerifyEmail.svelte:26`, `about/Policy.svelte:120`.
- Fix: add `--spot-free-ink` and `--cta-ink` tokens (like the existing `--spot-booked-ink`). Light mode keeps the current foreground values. Dark mode uses about L 0.80 tints of the hue. Swap the listed sites to the ink tokens, or apply the `dark:text-spot-free` / `dark:text-cta` pattern already used in `lib/status.ts`.

**P1-10. Promised transactional emails are missing.**
- Evidence: `Emails.cs` only has Verify, SessionEnded, ReservationReminder, RefundPaid and ClaimDecision. PRODUCT.md promises booking confirmation and payment receipts, plus no-show and overstay notices. Drivers who book get no email and no PDF link.
- Fix: add `BookingConfirmed`, with the lot, bay, lot-local window, plate, total, refund rule and receipt link. Also add `NoShow` and `Overstay`, with what happened, the charge and how to dispute. Reuse the existing layout, the plain-text twin and the escaping.

**P1-11. Booking bay map tiles are about 42 px wide at 360 px.**
- Evidence: `components/SpotGrid.svelte:157` uses `grid-cols-6 gap-1` inside page `px-4`, Step `p-4` and panel `p-3`. (360 − 32 − 32 − 24 − 20) / 6 ≈ 42 px, below the DESIGN 44 px rule. It passes from 375 px. PRODUCT names 360 px as the floor.
- Fix: below 375 px use `grid-cols-5`, or drop the panel padding to `p-2` and the Step to `p-3` on phones. (This can drop to P2 if 360 px is not a real target.)

**P1-12. Focus is lost after Inertia visits.**
- Evidence: the pending-visit skeleton (`lib/pending-visit.svelte.ts`, `NavigationSkeleton.svelte`) replaces the content, so a focused element unmounts and focus falls to `<body>`. Nothing moves focus or announces the new page. `PageTitle` only sets `<title>`.
- Impact: keyboard and screen-reader users land at the top of the document with no announcement after every navigation.
- Fix: in `app.ts`, on `router.on('navigate')` (not on partial reloads), focus `#main` (with `tabindex="-1"`) or the page `h1`. Add a visually hidden `aria-live="polite"` route announcer that reads `document.title`.

### P2

1. **Time formatting is inconsistent.**
   - Unlabelled UTC appears in:
     - `account/Payments.svelte:54,75`
     - `account/Insurance.svelte:76,100`
     - admin Lot, Booking, User, Bookings, Refunds, Claim and Sessions (`admin/Sessions.svelte:63` says "All times are UTC")
     - `NotificationBell.svelte:37`, which appends "UTC"
   - Charts label days "Nov 18" (month first) while the app uses "18 Nov".
   - `lots/Show.svelte:36` uses an en-GB locale directly.
   - `admin/Claim.svelte:30` shows `incidentOn` (a date) with a time.
   - Fix: pass the lot tz everywhere a lot is known. Use the viewer's zone (with `zoneLabel`) for account-wide lists. Use `fmtDate` in chart ticks.
2. **A Button with `href` ignores `disabled`.**
   - `ui/button/Button.svelte:24-25` drops `disabled` for links, so "Lot is full" (`lots/Show.svelte:68,113,140`) stays a live link.
   - The concept is also wrong: occupancy now should not block a booking for tomorrow.
   - Fix: render `<span aria-disabled>` for disabled links. Change the copy to "Full right now, book a later time".
3. **Touch targets are under 44 px on phones.** DESIGN requires 44 px. Fix: an `sm` size that is h-11 below `md`, and `min-h-11` on the listed items.

   | Element | Location | Current size |
   |---|---|---|
   | Button `sm` | `ui/button/variants.ts:24` | h-10 |
   | Pagination | `Pagination.svelte:18,20` | min-h-10 |
   | Saved-vehicle chips | `BookingFlow.svelte:447` | min-h-10 |
   | Team "Remove access" | `Team.svelte:85` | h-8 |
   | Refund presets | `Refunds.svelte:159` | py-1 text-xs |
   | Home "Explore" links | `Home.svelte:54` | min-h-9 |
   | Dropdown items | `DropdownMenu.svelte:35` | py-1.5 |
   | Admin Sessions plate pill | | min-h-10 |
   | "Save your plates" link | | text-xs |

   Button `sm` is used for receipts, New booking, Mark all read, Add vehicle, Refunds Review/Mark paid and claim Approve/Reject.
4. **Duplicate Toaster on auth pages.** `layouts/AuthLayout.svelte:51` mounts its own `<Toaster position="bottom-right">` on top of the global AppToaster. Toasts render twice, and bottom-right on phones. Fix: delete the local Toaster.
5. **No forgot-password flow.** `app.html:23` already anticipates `/forgot` and `/reset`, but Login has no link and there is no route. Fix: add the flow, or remove the dead routing hint.
6. **Two "Mark all read" behaviours.** `account/Alerts.svelte:36` puts it behind a ConfirmDialog. The bell's version has none. Marking alerts read is cheap and reversible, so fix: drop the confirmation.
7. **Unread state is colour-only for screen readers.** `aria-label` on a plain `<span>` is ignored (`AlertList.svelte:69`, `NotificationBell.svelte:33`). Fix: use `<span class="sr-only">Unread:</span>`.
8. **ConfirmDialog has no processing feedback.** The customer cancel (`account/Booking.svelte:106`) can be double-confirmed. Fix: add a `processing` prop that disables the action and shows a spinner.
9. **The lot time-zone select is unsearchable.** It lists about 420 IANA zones in a plain select (`LotForm.svelte:22,112`). Fix: use a Combobox with type-ahead, and put common zones first.
10. **AdminLayout attention badges go stale.** `AdminLayout.svelte:20-24` only refreshes them on the overview. Fix: make them a live prop, or refresh them on `navigate`.
11. **Lots index on phones.**
    - The filter card sits above the results (`lots/Index.svelte:63-89`), so the lots fall below the fold. Fix: collapse the filters into a "Filters (n)" Sheet trigger.
    - `maxPrice` is carried but has no UI.
    - "N parking locations loaded" has no singular form.
12. **Lot Show phone bar.**
    - `aria-live` on the free count (`Show.svelte:136`) announces every live change. Fix: announce only on user action.
    - "Book a bay" appears three times on a phone.
13. **The booking detail rail is missing information.** It has no address, phone, arrival notes or lot link (`account/Booking.svelte:302-308`). That is exactly what a driver needs on arrival.
14. **Raw codes appear in UI copy.**
    - The guard manual bay shows `manual.type` ("handicap") at `Console.svelte:220`.
    - Admin Lot shows `selected.type` at line 118.
    - The claim kind `replace('_',' ')` produces "Brokenwindow" (`admin/Insurance.svelte:64`).
    - The ClaimDecision email gets the raw kind (`EmailJobHandlers.cs:101`).
    - Fix: one `labels.ts` map shared by the client, plus a matching C# map.
15. **Policy page copy and links.**
    - The colour words "mint/violet" do not match what users see (green/blue).
    - The "materialized into individual occurrences" jargon remains.
    - The quick links point to the same page or to itself.
    - `hover:border-cta` and ochre icon chips on links break *Ochre Is Not A Button*.
    - A kicker sits above the h1.
16. **Admin Claim detail.**
    - It cannot decide `under_review` claims.
    - The description shows a raw kind plus a GUID.
    - It uses bordered cards.
    - Errors are listed at the top, away from their fields.
17. **admin/User status column shows raw lowercase strings.** Fix: use `StatusBadge`.
18. **Email and receipt terminology does not match the UI.** They say "spot" and "window" ("Recurring, N windows", "24h+ lead") where the UI says "bay" and "booking". RefundPaid shows only a ShortId with no lot or date. The email button reads "Open your insurance".
19. **BookingFlow tablists lack keyboard and panel wiring.** The tablists (`BookingFlow.svelte:298`) have no arrow-key handling and no `aria-controls`/tabpanel. The `disabledReason` `role=alert` (line 507) duplicates the inline alerts, so it is announced twice.
20. **The guard sticky header is tall on phones.** With the full-width lot select it takes about 130 px of a 640 px viewport. Fix: put the lot in a compact header chip that opens a Sheet.
21. **Light-mode Switch track fails non-text contrast.** The unchecked track (`bg-input` on card) is 1.54:1, below the 3:1 minimum (`ui/switch/Switch.svelte:12`). Fix: a darker unchecked track (about L 0.72) or a 1 px border.

## What works (keep it)

- **BookingFlow:**
  - The window-aware bay map: debounced reload, pending dim, `aria-busy`, and a "bay was taken" notice.
  - `refundRule` uses the real cutoffs.
  - Lot-local time with `zonedToUtc` handles DST gaps and overlaps correctly.
- **Loading:**
  - The nav skeleton system and the boot shell.
  - The Deferred fallback and rescue pattern on lot Show and Analytics.
- **Optimistic UI:** preference switches are serialised to avoid races, and mark-read is optimistic.
- **Gate console:** big targets, a shake animation that respects reduced motion, and an `aria-live` result line.
- **Emails:** dark mode, a plain-text twin and correct escaping.

## Persona red flags

- **Guard on a phone at a New York lot (Console, Incidents):** reads times 5 hours off, is told to resolve overstays for cars inside their window, and resolves incidents without seeing the description.
- **Admin triaging claims on a phone:** a note leaks across claims, Reject fires on one tap, and the bay inspector opens below the fold.
- **Screen-reader driver:** focus is dropped on every navigation, unread alerts are colour-only, and the BookingFlow tabs do not support arrow keys.
- **Dark-mode driver:** the overstay warning and the "Daily max applied" saving are near-invisible (about 1.2 to 1.4:1).
- **New driver after booking:** no confirmation email, and forgetting the password has no way back.
